## Short description (about 30 words)

wheretopark.sg tells Singapore drivers what parking will actually cost before they arrive. Search a destination and see nearby carparks ranked by price for your stay, with live free lots.

## Section paragraph (about 70 words)

Parking prices in Singapore are scattered across HDB, URA, LTA, malls and private operators, and they change by day and time. wheretopark.sg brings them together. Search where you're going and it ranks nearby carparks by the estimated cost of your stay, shows live free lots, the walk to your destination and height limits, then hands off to Google Maps or Waze. It runs on the web, on Android and on Android Auto.

## Case study (about 200 words)

**The problem.** It started at a football field. For Brahmasta, a father of two, finding parking while his child played football was a recurring struggle. Singapore publishes a lot of parking data, but it is spread across agencies and operators, in different formats, and rates change by day type and hour.

**What was built.** wheretopark.sg combines ten data sources, from HDB and URA rate schedules and LTA's live availability feed to mall rates and EV chargers, into one answer: the cheapest carpark near your destination for the time you plan to stay. More than 3,800 carparks are mapped, with over 22,000 rate rows priced by day type, hour and public holiday.

**How it was built.** The product treats the credibility of the number as the product. When data is missing it says so: an unknown count shows a dash instead of a false "Full", stale rates are labelled and can't win "Cheapest", and carparks without rates say "Rate unknown" instead of guessing. Drivers can report wrong rates and suggest corrections.

**Where it is now.** Live on the web, in closed testing on Google Play, with an Android Auto version for the car's screen.

## Feature highlights

1. **Cheapest first.** Nearby carparks ranked by the estimated cost of your stay, not just distance.
2. **Live free lots.** Real-time availability where the carpark publishes it, and an honest dash where it doesn't.
3. **Plan your stay.** Set when you arrive and how long you'll stay; weekday, weekend, evening and public-holiday rates are applied.
4. **Walk to your destination.** Walking time and route from the carpark to where you're going.
5. **For every vehicle.** Filters for EV charging, motorcycle lots and heavy vehicles, plus height limits.
6. **In the car.** Android Auto: search a destination by voice or keyboard, see prices and free lots on the car's screen, and start navigation.

## Claims to avoid

- Don't call it "every carpark in Singapore". Say "more than 3,800 carparks".
- Don't promise live availability everywhere. Say "live free lots where the carpark publishes them".
- Don't present usage figures publicly without approval.
- Don't call it official or government-run. It uses public government data and is independent.
- iOS is in development; don't say it's on the App Store.
